The problem, the plan and what changed.

Blossom case studies document more than the finished photograph. They show what the client needed, how the work was organised and the difference the completed result made.

Case study structure

What every case study will record

  • The property and garden
  • What the client needed
  • Why the existing position was not working
  • The scope Blossom accepted
  • The plan and design decisions
  • The specialists involved
  • The budget band, where the client permits publication
  • The delivery programme
  • Issues encountered and how they were resolved
  • The completed result
  • The ongoing management plan
  • The client's view, published only with permission

The first case studies will be added when real Blossom work can be documented honestly. No stock project will be presented as client evidence.
