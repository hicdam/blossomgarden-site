Headley Patio Transformation

A tired and disconnected patio transformed into a welcoming outdoor space for everyday living.

The Challenge

The existing patio felt dated and separate from the garden. The layout provided little reason to spend time outside and did not make the best use of the space immediately behind the house.

What We Created

We introduced a clean, contemporary patio with generous space for outdoor dining and relaxed seating. Defined edges, considered planting and a stronger connection to the lawn helped the whole garden feel more coherent.

The Result

A practical and inviting outdoor living area that feels like a natural extension of the home, with space to eat, relax and entertain.

Haslemere Pergola And Dining Garden

A simple garden redesigned around outdoor dining and time spent together.

The Challenge

Although the garden had usable space, it lacked structure, shade and a comfortable setting for entertaining. The dining area felt exposed and disconnected from the rest of the garden.

What We Created

A timber pergola now defines the dining space and gives it a stronger sense of enclosure. Climbing plants, surrounding borders and carefully positioned lighting soften the structure and help it settle naturally into the garden.

The Result

A welcoming outdoor room with a clear purpose, offering a comfortable place for family meals, entertaining and relaxed evenings outside.

Liphook Garden Room And Outbuilding

A generous new garden room designed to complement an existing outbuilding and create a flexible space for everyday life.

The Challenge

The existing outbuilding had character and suited its rural garden setting, but it did not provide the comfortable, year-round space the owners needed.

The challenge was to introduce a substantially larger garden room without making the two buildings feel unrelated or allowing the new structure to overpower its surroundings.

The Result

A spacious and versatile garden room that feels as though it belongs to the property.

The new and existing buildings now work together as one coherent group, creating a sheltered courtyard and a flexible space that can be used for work, exercise, hobbies or relaxed time with family and friends.

Surrey Hills Stargazing Cabin

A secluded observatory cabin for an amateur astronomer living in a semi-rural Surrey Hills home.

Before it was an unused area at the end of the garden with patchy grass, an old timber shed and partially overgrown access.

Now it is a warm study and equipment-storage area, a small adjoining observation section with a retractable roof over the observation area.

Farnham Decking Retreat

An overlooked corner transformed into a warm and comfortable place to unwind.

The Challenge

The garden lacked a clearly defined seating area. The available space was underused and needed a solution that would feel comfortable, attractive and connected to the surrounding planting.

What We Created

We designed a raised timber deck with space for relaxed seating, softened by layered planting around its edges. The materials bring warmth to the garden while the planting creates privacy and a more intimate atmosphere.

The Result

A calm garden retreat that gives the space a clear purpose and provides an inviting place for morning coffee, quiet afternoons and summer evenings.
