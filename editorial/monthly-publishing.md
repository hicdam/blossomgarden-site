# Monthly Garden Journal publishing

Authorised by Damo on 24 September 2026. Use Europe/London dates.

## On the 1st

- Publish the current month's journal article. October 2026 copy and image are in editorial/october-2026.md. For later months, use an existing relevant editorial draft; if none exists, prepare a contextual monthly guide of no more than 500 words using verified RHS advice and only the supplied stock assets listed in assets/img/journal-stock/sources.json. Retain sources in the editorial file. Do not invent weather forecasts, business claims or prices.
- Use the current article template, semantic subheadings, short paragraphs and practical checklists. No captions, drop caps or forced wrapping. Include maintenance and enquiry links.
- Add the new article first in journal.html immediately, with the real publication date, correct title, summary, image and link. Retain the archive. Update article metadata, canonical URL and sitemap.
- Replace the homepage's monthly-homepage-hero:start/end region with a prominent feature for this article. Use its relevant supplied stock image, heading, short summary and Read the [month] guide button. Retain a secondary route to services or the project enquiry.
- Before replacement, save the current ordinary homepage hero in editorial/homepage-default-hero.html so the latest homepage copy can be restored. Never overwrite this backup with a previous journal promotion.
- Keep the previous month's maintenance feature until the 8th. Do not change other homepage sections.

## On the 8th

- Verify the current month's article is published and linked in the journal.
- Restore the default homepage hero from editorial/homepage-default-hero.html, preserving the marker comments and any unrelated recent edits. Only replace an active monthly journal hero; do not overwrite a newly edited ordinary hero.
- Replace the maintenance monthly-journal-feature:start/end block with the current month's article, contextual image, summary and links. Keep it featured until the following month's 8th, so maintenance is never left blank during the next homepage week.
- Keep the latest article first in journal.html and all earlier articles accessible. Do not unpublish the article when moving its feature.

- Report successful publication or handover briefly. Operations must be safe to rerun without duplicate articles or cards.
