The garden is part of the property

Looking after it should not become another job for you.

A garden can involve designers, gardeners, landscapers, arborists, electricians, irrigation engineers, pool specialists, furniture makers and building suppliers. Each solves one part. Someone still has to understand the whole, make the decisions connect and keep everything moving.

Blossom becomes that single point of responsibility. We can solve one immediate problem, run a defined project, transform the entire garden or manage it continuously throughout the year.

You decide what you want from the garden. We take care of making it happen.

Done for you

One brief. One plan. One person keeping hold of the whole.

See how Blossom works

  1. Tell us what you need

    It can be a finished idea, a persistent problem or simply the feeling that the garden is not working as it should.

  2. We establish the right solution

    We assess the garden, define the requirement, identify constraints and give you a clear route with realistic costs and decisions.

  3. We assemble and manage the delivery

    We source suitable specialists, compare proposals, coordinate the programme, manage communication and protect the quality of the result.

  4. We remain accountable

    We close defects, organise aftercare and can continue managing the garden once the work is complete.

Your Garden Manager

The garden has someone looking after the whole of it.

Maintenance is only one part of garden management. Your Garden Manager oversees the people, plans, systems and decisions that keep the garden working and improving.

  • Annual garden plan and priorities
  • Gardener and contractor coordination
  • Seasonal work programme
  • Routine garden inspections
  • Quote and invoice review
  • Plant, tree and lawn care planning
  • Lighting, irrigation, pond and pool servicing
  • Reactive problem management
  • Warranty and defect tracking
  • Future improvement planning
