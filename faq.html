Tell us about your project

Quick answers

What work do you do?

We deliver hard landscaping projects. Garden buildings, decking, gates, fencing and maintenance are our specialist areas. We also help with patios, paths, walls and pergolas.

Do you offer garden maintenance?

Yes. We can help with regular garden care, seasonal tidy-ups and upkeep of outdoor spaces. We agree the jobs, visit frequency and price before work starts. You can book maintenance without a building project. Explore garden maintenance.

Can you do just one job?

Yes. You do not need a whole-garden redesign or an ongoing agreement.

Do I need a Garden Review first?

No. A clear, straightforward job can start with a conversation. A review helps if you are unsure what the garden needs.

How much will it cost?

The price depends on the work, materials, access and site conditions. We confirm costs in writing before you commit. A Garden Review is a fixed £295; other service pages explain their guide fees.

Who carries out the work?

We organise the people your project needs. Appointed contractors and specialists remain responsible for their own work and warranties.

Can you work with my architect, builder or gardener?

Yes. We agree who is doing what and coordinate the garden work around them.

Can we do the work in stages?

Yes. We can agree a wider plan and split it into manageable projects.

Will I need permissions?

Requirements depend on your property and the work. We help identify where qualified advice or approvals are needed before the project starts.

Where do you work?

Surrey, Hampshire and Sussex. Send your postcode so we can confirm coverage.

What happens after I enquire?

We get in touch to discuss your idea and agree the next step. The first call is free.
