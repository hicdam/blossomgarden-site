For architects & builders

A reliable garden and external-works partner for residential projects.

Blossom can own the garden brief, specialist coordination and post-build garden handover without blurring the responsibilities of the main project team.

The role

A garden and external-works partner that supports the main project rather than competing with it.

Blossom can join a residential project where the garden, external works and post-build landscape need an accountable owner. We coordinate the garden brief, relevant specialists, suppliers and delivery while respecting the architect's, contractor's and consultant team's defined responsibilities.

For architects

Protect the design intent without inheriting every garden decision.

  • Translate the client's garden requirements into a clear external brief
  • Coordinate landscape, planting and garden specialists
  • Support supplier research and comparison
  • Manage garden-room, irrigation, lighting and outdoor-living packages
  • Keep landscape decisions aligned with the wider property design
  • Coordinate establishment and ongoing garden management after handover

The architect remains responsible for the architectural services they are appointed to provide. Blossom does not take over that role.

For builders

Take the specialist garden package out of the main build team's queue.

  • Define and coordinate external works that sit beyond the core build package
  • Source appropriate garden and landscape specialists
  • Coordinate planting, irrigation, lighting and garden structures
  • Manage handover, snagging and specialist warranties
  • Provide the homeowner with a route into ongoing management
  • Reduce late-stage garden decisions landing back on the site team

The principal contractor and each trade remain responsible for their own contractual and statutory duties.

Where Blossom can join

Early enough to prevent rework, or late enough to close the gap.

Concept

Garden brief

Clarify how the client wants to use the garden and what needs to connect to the house, services and wider design.

Pre-construction

Scope and procurement

Define packages, identify specialist input, obtain comparable proposals and make dependencies visible before site work locks decisions in.

Construction

Coordination

Coordinate garden specialists, information, decisions, access, sequencing and client approvals alongside the main project team.

Handover

Establishment and care

Close snags, gather care and warranty information, establish maintenance and give the client one route for what happens next.

Commercial position

Clear boundaries and no hidden referral economics.

Blossom's role, fee and decision authority are agreed with the client or appointing party before work begins. If any supplier commission, procurement margin or referral arrangement applies, it is disclosed rather than hidden inside the recommendation.
