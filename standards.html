Our commitments

Agree before starting

We set out the work, fees, responsibilities and exclusions in writing.

Keep you in control

You approve the budget and significant changes. Any supplier fees or commissions are disclosed.

Choose suitable people

We consider experience, availability, insurance where needed, and value. We can work with your existing trades.

Check the work

We record unfinished work and problems, agree who will address them and follow up.

Respect specialist roles

Appointed contractors and professionals remain responsible for their work and warranties.

Avoid unnecessary work

Where it makes sense, we repair or improve what is already there.
