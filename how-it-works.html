How it works

1. Tell us your idea

Tell us about your project or maintenance needs and your location. The first call is free.

2. Agree the plan and price

We confirm the work, costs and timing in writing. For maintenance, we agree the jobs and visit frequency. A site visit or design may be needed first.

3. We organise the work

We coordinate the team, keep you updated and check the finished work against the plan.

You stay in control

You approve the budget and significant changes. Any design or review fee is agreed before booking.

A simple project can start with a conversation. You do not have to buy a Garden Review first.
