Tell us about your project

Three clear stages

1. Plan your garden

Agree the layout, features and budget, with any surveys or specialist advice needed.

2. Agree the work

Confirm suppliers, costs and timing before construction starts.

3. Build and finish

We coordinate the work, keep you updated and arrange final checks and care information.

What does it cost?

Every garden is different.

Your budget should reflect what you want the garden to give you: useful space, a finish you love and care you can manage. Read how we approach garden investment and value.

We agree the design, scope and budget with you, then confirm the costs and what’s included in writing before work begins.

You approve major decisions and significant changes. Appointed specialists remain responsible for their own work.
