Meet Blossom.

Garden design, build and maintenance. Creative thinking, practical skills and care for the long term.

Tell us about your project

Damian Hickey

Hi, I’m Damian, founder of Blossom. We design, build and maintain gardens and outdoor spaces for homes and local businesses.

My career began in graphic and exhibition design, before moving into creative studio leadership and bringing design, technology and delivery teams together. Over more than 30 years, I’ve helped turn ideas into finished products and services, starting with what people need and how they will use them.

At Blossom, that design background shapes how we think about your garden: the layout, proportions, materials and details, and how the space feels and works in everyday life. We start with your ideas, your home and your budget, then bring the possibilities into focus.

I combine that creative background with hands-on landscaping and carpentry skills, and experience managing budgets, suppliers and teams. A good design needs to be practical to build. We consider access, materials and the order of work early, so your budget goes into the result rather than avoidable delays, waste or rework.

I’ve also led global smart facilities management at one of the world’s largest facilities management businesses. That brings an understanding of how to keep spaces safe, useful and well cared for long after the initial work is finished. We consider future upkeep while designing and building, and can provide regular maintenance as your garden grows.

Whether you need a new gate, a planting refresh, ongoing care or a complete garden transformation, you get the same thoughtful approach. Clear costs, a safe and tidy working area, and one point of contact from the first idea through to the care that follows.
