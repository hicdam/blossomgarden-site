Meet Blossom.

Tell us about your project

Damian Hickey

Hi, I’m Damian. I’m a practical maker with more than 30 years’ experience leading projects, hands-on skills in landscaping and carpentry, and a lifelong interest in gardens and nature.

My background includes leading smart facilities management at one of the world’s largest facilities management businesses. That experience taught me that a successful project needs more than a good idea. It needs a realistic budget, careful use of time and materials, and the right people doing the work in the right order.

At Blossom, I apply those same principles to landscaping and garden care. We look at access, materials and practical constraints early, agree what your budget needs to achieve and organise the work to reduce avoidable delays, waste and extra costs.

That approach matters just as much for a new gate or regular maintenance as it does for a complete garden transformation. You don’t need a large project to benefit from thoughtful planning and care.

We agree the work and price before starting, keep you informed and discuss changes before they become extra costs. Throughout the work, we respect your property and keep the working area safe and tidy.

One point of contact, from the first conversation through to completion.
