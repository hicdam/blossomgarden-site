Garden buildings

Start with the cladding: natural grain, a softer painted finish or a darker contrast.

Natural timber cladding

Warm timber, visible grain and vertical lines.

Care & things to consider

Uncoated timber changes colour as it weathers. Exposure and shade can make that change uneven.

Choose the timber species and decide whether you want it to weather or keep a coated finish.

Sage-painted cladding

A muted green finish with horizontal boards.

Care & things to consider

Painted timber needs its coating maintained in line with the coating manufacturer’s guidance.

Check a painted sample outside, alongside your planting and existing brickwork.

Charcoal-painted cladding

Dark boards with a strong vertical rhythm.

Care & things to consider

A coated finish needs ongoing care. We would confirm a suitable coating system for the chosen timber.

Compare the dark finish in sun and shade before settling on a colour.

Decking

Compare warm timber with grey and dark wood-effect finishes.

Natural timber decking

Warm boards with grain and variation.

Care & things to consider

A specialist finish can add colour or help manage weathering. Its care depends on the timber and coating chosen.

Discuss board profile, cleaning access and the appearance you want as the deck ages.

Timber decking guidance (opens in a new tab) ↗

Grey wood-effect decking

A softer grey palette with a wood-grain appearance.

Care & things to consider

Millboard’s Enhanced Grain range needs cleaning but does not require routine sanding, staining or oiling.

Explore Smoked Oak as a product example. Compare a real sample with your paving.
