Clear costs before work starts

We confirm the work, price and payment stages in writing. You approve significant changes before we proceed.

Project management fees

Where separate project management is needed, guide fees start at £950 a month while work is on site, or 10% of the build cost for full management from choosing contractors to completion. We agree the basis before starting.

Appointed contractors remain responsible for their work and warranties.
